Privacy policy

For JaarZon 2.7 · Last updated: 18 November 2026

Your privacy in JaarZon. Solar calculations take place locally. Location permission is optional; place names are not requested online. Below we explain what the app stores and how you can delete it.

1. About JaarZon

JaarZon is developed by Tobie Doolaard, a private individual using the developer name Tobie Apps. The app calculates day length, sunrise, sunset and Golden Hour for a location. These calculations, charts and time zone calculations take place on your device and work without an internet connection.

This policy describes the current app. Visiting this web page is separate from the offline calculations.

2. Location and your choice

JaarZon can ask Android for an approximate or precise location. The coordinates are used to calculate daylight information and identify the time zone where it can be recognised offline. JaarZon does not pass coordinates on to request a place name.

Location permission is optional. You can choose approximate location, deny permission or revoke it later in Android’s app settings. Without a new location, JaarZon uses a usable last known location, or otherwise Amsterdam, Netherlands as the default.

The app requests location only while in use, with a bounded request of about ten seconds. Its own location request stops when the app is paused. JaarZon does not request background location permission and has no background location service.

3. Data processed and stored by the app

  • One last known location: latitude, longitude, reported accuracy and the time of the location fix.
  • A previously stored place name, if available: a locality or area name and country from an older app version. This name is used only for the same stored coordinates. For other coordinates JaarZon displays the coordinates; the app does not request new place names.
  • Preferences: language choice and a flag recording that the location explanation has been shown. On recent Android versions, Android manages the app language preference.
  • Temporary screen state: information such as the selected date and current location may be retained by Android when the screen is recreated.

The app record is held in private local app storage. A newly accepted location replaces the previous location record; the app does not maintain a route or location history. Displayed coordinates may be rounded; stored coordinates are not rounded in the same way.

4. Retention and deletion

The stored last known location has no automatic expiry date. It may remain until replaced by a new location or until you delete the app data. The language preference and location explanation flag remain locally until changed or removed.

Revoking location permission does not delete previously stored data. The app may still use the saved location as ‘last known’, displaying the time of the fix.

You can delete local app data through Android Settings → Apps → JaarZon → Storage → Clear data / Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app. Menu names vary by device. Clearing the cache alone is not sufficient. You can also change Android’s own app language preference in Android’s language settings.

This does not necessarily delete data already processed by a system provider. The app cannot guarantee those providers’ retention periods or deletion options.

5. Android system services and possible external processing

JaarZon uses Android LocationManager for automatic location, with your permission and only while in use. The app no longer calls Android Geocoder and has no server or external requests of its own for place-name lookup.

Depending on your device, system provider and settings, Android location services may use network services. JaarZon cannot establish or guarantee which data these services process, retain or delete externally. JaarZon's lack of internet permission does not rule out network access by separate system services.

Astronomical calculations and time zone identification take place locally. The default location is Amsterdam, Netherlands. Previously stored place names may originate from an older app version that used Geocoder; this change does not delete any data processed externally at that time.

6. Security and other data flows

The app uses private Android app storage. Android Auto Backup is disabled; the configured transfer rules also exclude app data from cloud backup and device migration. This describes the app configuration, not the behaviour of separate system providers.

The local WebView blocks network traffic and loads only bundled files. The current app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking libraries, accounts or upload features of its own. No app-written location logging statements were found in the app code. This does not describe logs maintained by Android or its providers.

We make no unverified promise about encryption, storage or onward disclosure by an Android location provider.

7. Support messages

When you email us, you voluntarily provide your email address, the content of your message and any name or attachments you include. We use support messages solely to handle questions, reports and any follow-up. Please provide only information needed for that purpose.

We retain this correspondence for no more than 12 months after the last correspondence, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. You can request deletion of your correspondence at tobieapps.support@proton.me. A legal retention obligation may prevent complete deletion.

This is the retention policy to be followed by Tobie Apps; it is not an automatic deletion feature enforced by JaarZon. Clearing app data does not delete email correspondence.

Support is handled through our Proton email address. Your own email provider and Proton are involved in sending and receiving messages. See Proton’s privacy information for processing by that service. We make no unverified promises about email providers’ transport security or retention periods.

8. Your rights and contact

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. You can revoke your Android location permission at any time. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority.

For questions, support and privacy requests, you can contact Tobie Doolaard (Tobie Apps) at tobieapps.support@proton.me. You can delete local data as described above; JaarZon provides no feature allowing the developer to remotely inspect or erase your local app storage.

If you visit this page through GitHub Pages, GitHub provides the hosting. The page contains no scripts, analytics, forms or external fonts of its own. GitHub’s processing of website traffic is separate from local app calculations; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The exact hosting processing is not a property of the Android source code.
